What it is

The bits&bytes Overclock Hackathon is a dedicated competition within Techfest, IIT Bombay. It runs as regional qualifiers (zonals) across India, and Kolkata is hosting one of them.

How it goes

  1. 01 · The Kolkata regional. 12 hours on a Kolkata campus, 8 AM to 8 PM.
  2. 02 · The best teams win. The strongest teams from the regional are selected as winners.
  3. 03 · The final at Techfest. Winners go to IIT Bombay and compete in the final.

Hosting institutes: what we ask for

  • A venue for 12 hours, 8 AM to 8 PM, on an agreed date
  • A main hall and auditorium for the opening, demos and judging
  • Stable Wi-Fi, adequate power, basic AV (projector, mic, screen)
  • Free drinking water and hygienic washrooms
  • Permission for banners and signage, registration and partner table space
  • Reserved seats for your students (100-150 max) and a point of contact on the day
